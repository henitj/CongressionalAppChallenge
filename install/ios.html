Add it to your Home Screen

  1. Open this site in Safari.

    Other iPhone browsers cannot add the Home Screen app.

  2. Tap Share

    It is the square with an arrow pointing up.

  3. Tap “Add to Home Screen”, then Add.
While recording, keep EcoTrek open on screen. iPhone may pause a web app in the background. Allow location when Safari asks so your route can be recorded.
Will my walks and settings be saved?

Yes. They are saved on this device in Safari. Clearing Safari’s website data for EcoTrek also clears its saved app data.

Can I use it offline?

The app can open offline and locally saved activities remain available. Live trail search and weather need an internet connection.
