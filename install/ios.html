Android app

Install EcoTrek on Android.

Download the app on your phone, open the file, then tap Install. Android may ask you to allow the install once.

↓ Download EcoTrekAndroid app · APK Open web appInstall from Chrome—no APK

Download the file on the Android phone where you want to use the app.

Install in three steps

  1. Open the download.

    When it finishes, tap the download notification or open Files → Downloads → EcoTrek.apk.

  2. Allow the install, if asked.

    Tap Settings, allow installs from this browser or Files app, then go back and open the APK again.

  3. Tap Install, then Open.

    EcoTrek will appear with your other apps.

Install from the website instead

If Android rejects the APK, EcoTrek also runs as an installable web app—no APK or sideloading required.

  1. Tap “Open web app” above

    Open it in Chrome on your Android phone.

  2. Open Chrome’s menu.

    Tap ⋮ → Install app or Add to Home screen (the wording varies by Android version).

  3. Launch EcoTrek from your Home screen.

    Your activities are saved in that browser on this device. Live trail and weather features need internet.

Android does not show Install, or says “App not installed”
  • If nothing opens, allow Install unknown apps for the browser or Files app you used, then open EcoTrek.apk again.
  • If Android says “App not installed” or “package appears to be invalid”, delete any saved EcoTrek.apk and download it again from this page. A copy downloaded before this release keeps its old package metadata.
  • Make sure the download completed. This file is about 1.8 MB and needs Android 7.0 or newer.
  • If EcoTrek is already installed from another APK, Android may refuse an update signed with a different key. Uninstalling that older copy may allow this one to install, but will erase its locally saved walks and settings. Do not uninstall it unless you are prepared to lose that data; use the web app above instead if unsure.

If it still fails, email us the exact error text, your phone model and Android version: ecotrek.support@gmail.com.

Play Protect says it does not recognize the app

This can happen with apps installed outside Google Play. If you choose to continue, tap More details → Install anyway when Android offers that choice. Keep Play Protect on. If Android blocks the install without that option, use the browser version instead.

Will my walks and settings be saved?

Yes. They are saved on this device in Safari. Clearing Safari’s website data for EcoTrek also clears its saved app data.

Can I use it offline?

The app can open offline and locally saved activities remain available. Live trail search and weather need an internet connection.

Optional setup

The assistant works without a key.

EcoTrek already answers trail questions on your device. Add an AI key only if you want a model to polish those answers. Walks, trails, weather, points and badges do not need one.

Just want to use EcoTrek? Skip this tab. Nothing is locked, and you can add a key later.

Three simple steps

Want to turn on AI answers?

Groq is the quickest setup. It offers a free tier; check its current limits and terms before creating a key.

  1. Create a Groq key

    Make a key on Groq’s website, then copy it.

    Get a key ↗
  2. Open AI settings

    In EcoTrek, tap the sparkle button to open Trail Assistant, then tap the sliders icon and choose Groq.

  3. Paste and test

    Paste your key and tap Save & test. The provider address and model are filled in for you.

Keep your key private. It stays on your device and is sent directly to the AI provider you choose over HTTPS. EcoTrek does not receive it.

Your own server

Choose Custom in AI settings and enter your OpenAI-compatible server, model name and key. The address must use HTTPS.

What happens to my key and my questions?

The key is saved in the app’s local storage. When you ask for an AI answer, EcoTrek sends your question and relevant trail or weather facts, with the key, directly to your selected provider over HTTPS. The provider’s own privacy terms apply to that request.

EcoTrek does not send the key to our server. You can remove it at any time in Trail Assistant → AI settings → Remove key. If you think it was exposed, revoke it on the provider’s website.

My key will not connect

Check that you selected the same provider that issued the key, then copy and paste it again. A provider may also reject an expired key or apply a usage limit. The assistant will keep working with its on-device answers either way.

If you still need help, email ecotrek.support@gmail.com. Do not include your key.
