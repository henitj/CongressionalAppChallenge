A few good-to-knows

Questions, answered.

How do I install the APK?

Tap either download button on an Android phone. When the download finishes, open the file, review the permission prompt, and tap Install. You only need to do the browser permission step the first time.

Will my progress be saved?

Yes. EcoTrek saves activities, points, badges, streaks, and settings locally on your phone. If Google sign-in and cloud sync are enabled for the build, signing in lets your account restore supported progress on another device.

Does EcoTrek track me all the time?

No. Location is requested only when you start recording an activity. Read the privacy policy for the full details.

Can I install this on an iPhone?

The downloadable package on this page is an Android APK. APK files cannot be installed on iPhone. An iOS build needs Apple distribution through the App Store or TestFlight.
