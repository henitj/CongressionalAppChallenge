Optional setup

The assistant works without a key.

EcoTrek already answers trail questions on your device. Add an AI key only if you want a model to polish those answers. Walks, trails, weather, points and badges do not need one.

Just want to use EcoTrek? Skip this page. Nothing is locked, and you can add a key later.

Three simple steps

Want to turn on AI answers?

Groq is the quickest setup. It offers a free tier; check its current limits and terms before creating a key.

  1. Create a Groq key

    Make a key on Groq’s website, then copy it.

    Get a key ↗
  2. Open AI settings

    In EcoTrek, tap the sparkle button to open Trail Assistant, then tap the sliders icon and choose Groq.

  3. Paste and test

    Paste your key and tap Save & test. The provider address and model are filled in for you.

Keep your key private. It stays on your device and is sent directly to the AI provider you choose over HTTPS. EcoTrek does not receive it.

Your own server

Choose Custom in AI settings and enter your OpenAI-compatible server, model name and key. The address must use HTTPS.

What happens to my key and my questions?

The key is saved in the app’s local storage. When you ask for an AI answer, EcoTrek sends your question and relevant trail or weather facts, with the key, directly to your selected provider over HTTPS. The provider’s own privacy terms apply to that request.

EcoTrek does not send the key to our server. You can remove it at any time in Trail Assistant → AI settings → Remove key. If you think it was exposed, revoke it on the provider’s website.

My key will not connect

Check that you selected the same provider that issued the key, then copy and paste it again. A provider may also reject an expired key or apply a usage limit. The assistant will keep working with its on-device answers either way.

If you still need help, email ecotrek.support@gmail.com. Do not include your key.
