Privacy

Your outdoor data stays yours.

Last updated: October 7, 2026

EcoTrek is an outdoor activity tracker made for the Congressional App Challenge. This policy explains what is saved on your device and what is sent to other services.

The short version: The version on this site does not require an account. Your profile, walks and settings are saved on your device, not uploaded to EcoTrek. There are no ads or third-party tracking tools.

What is saved on your device

Your profile name, activity routes and stats, points, badges, streaks, settings and a small app-usage log are stored locally. The usage log helps the app keep track of feature use; it is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing the website’s data can remove this information.

Location and online features

EcoTrek may use your location to find nearby trails, show local conditions and record a walk, hike or ride. Location is used while you are using those features; the Android app does not request background location access.

To provide live information, your device may contact:

  • OpenStreetMap services for nearby trails and place names. A location is included in a nearby-trails lookup.
  • Open-Meteo and the U.S. National Weather Service for weather and safety information. The request uses your location to find local conditions.
  • Wikimedia Commons for some trail photos, searched by trail or place name.

These services receive the information needed to answer each request under their own privacy policies. EcoTrek does not send your name or saved route history with these lookups.

Optional AI key

The Trail Assistant works without a key. If you add your own key, it is stored on your device and sent directly over HTTPS to the AI provider you select. For an AI request, that provider receives your question and relevant trail or weather facts. Its privacy terms apply. EcoTrek does not receive the key.

You can remove the key in Trail Assistant → AI settings → Remove key. If it may have been exposed, revoke it through the provider. See the simple AI setup guide.

Your choices

  • Choose whether to allow location in your phone or browser settings.
  • Erase your saved app data in Settings → Your data, or remove the app.
  • Use the assistant without an AI key, or remove a key at any time.

Children

EcoTrek is intended for people age 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Future versions and contact

The release offered on this site stores app data locally and has no sign-in or cloud sync. A separately configured build could add online account features; its privacy information should explain any changes before use.

Questions or privacy concerns? Email ecotrek.support@gmail.com.
